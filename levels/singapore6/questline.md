# Singapore CBD Level Questline - Multiple Access Paths

## Initial Discovery Phase
Player begins with no specific objective, just looking for work. Any NPC will point toward the Tai Yong recruitment drive for "genetically compatible" subjects.

## Access Paths (Choose One)

### Path 1: Blackmail Leverage
**Key NPC:** Lin Mei Hua (Information Broker)
- Lin reveals that Guard Khai Chen's brother received illegal black market augmentations
- She offers footage proving the guard helped acquire these augmentations
- Player can confront Khai with this information and threaten to tell Sergeant Tan
- **Resolution:** "Here's the access key. Just keep quiet about my brother."

### Path 2: Corporate Identity Theft
**Key NPC:** Victoria Lim (Elite Socialite)
- Victoria mentions a Tai Yong executive arriving for the trials
- She knows their credentials but needs something in exchange
- Player must get dirt on Lin Mei Hua's corporate espionage activities
- **Resolution:** "Here's an executive access card. Megan won't know the difference - just don't make me regret this."

### Path 3: Medical Emergency Diversion
**Key NPCs:** Nika Zhang (Punk) & Sergeant Tan (Cop)
- Nika reveals her augmentations are malfunctioning from Reed's experiments
- Player convinces her to stage a distraction by collapsing near the tower entrance
- Must first convince Sergeant Tan to be in position to respond to the "emergency"
- When both are in position, Khai leaves his post to help, allowing entry
- **Resolution:** "I'll create a scene at exactly 14:00. Be ready to slip in when everyone's attention is on me."

### Path 4: Become a Test Subject Referral
**Key NPCs:** Sergeant Tan & Nika Zhang
- Learn from Sergeant Tan her sister works in recruitment
- Convince Nika to reveal her unique genetic markers that made her valuable
- Use this information to convince Tan to call her sister about a "perfect candidate"
- **Resolution:** "I've sent your details to my sister. She's excited about your profile. The guard will let you through now."

### Path 5: Corporate Espionage Contract
**Key NPC:** Lin Mei Hua
- Lin reveals she has a client who wants inside information on Project Chimera
- She offers legitimate credentials in exchange for agreeing to plant a data tap
- Player must convince Victoria to vouch for their reliability
- **Resolution:** "Here's a contractor badge. Remember our agreement - the data tap goes in Reed's terminal, not anywhere else."

## Execution Requirements

| Path | Required NPCs | Key Items | Special Notes |
|------|--------------|-----------|--------------|
| Blackmail | Lin Mei Hua, Khai Chen | "Incriminating Footage" | Requires threatening dialogue |
| Identity Theft | Victoria Lim, Lin Mei Hua | "Executive Access Card" | Requires corporate knowledge dialogue |
| Diversion | Nika Zhang, Sergeant Tan | None - timed event | Requires coordinating two NPCs |
| Referral | Sergeant Tan, Nika Zhang | None - internal recommendation | Most ethical approach |
| Espionage | Lin Mei Hua, Victoria Lim | "Contractor Badge" & "Data Tap" | Most aligned with Deus Ex themes |

## NPC Dialogue Tracking

Each NPC tracks key conversation branches:
- Information about Tai Yong recruitment (initial phase)
- Details specific to each access path
- Character backstory and world-building elements

The player unlocks progress by discovering connections between characters and leveraging these relationships to gain access through one of the multiple paths.

This system gives players genuine agency in how they approach the problem, fitting the Deus Ex philosophy of multiple solutions while maintaining the dialogue-only constraint.
