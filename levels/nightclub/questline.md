Nightclub Level - Tong's Lucky Money
Location Overview
A pulsing, neon-lit nightclub in Singapore's underground scene. Tong Si Hung, the aging crime boss, holds court in a private VIP area overlooking the dance floor. The club serves as a front for his various operations and a meeting place for his associates.
Story Connection
Following the player's recruitment at Taiyong Medical, Dr. Reed provides a final instruction before procedures begin: "You'll need to meet with a man named Tong Si Hung at his nightclub, the Lucky Money. He's a... financial partner in our research. Consider this your first assignment - he has a package that needs retrieval, and your natural appearance makes you perfect for the job."
